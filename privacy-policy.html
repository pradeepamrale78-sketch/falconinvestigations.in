Privacy Policy

Effective date: 10 September 2026

Falcon Investigations respects the privacy and confidentiality of individuals who contact us or use this website. This Privacy Policy explains what information may be collected, why it may be used, and how it is handled.

1. Information We May Collect

Depending on how you contact us, we may receive your name, telephone or WhatsApp number, email address, enquiry details, and other information you voluntarily provide in connection with a proposed investigation.

2. How We Use Information

  • To respond to enquiries and requests for investigation services.
  • To understand the scope and requirements of a proposed assignment.
  • To communicate regarding an enquiry, quotation, or service.
  • To maintain appropriate business and investigation records.
  • To comply with applicable legal or regulatory requirements.

3. Investigation Information

Information supplied by a client in relation to an investigation may be confidential and may be handled for legitimate investigation, reporting, communication, contractual, or legal purposes. Please avoid submitting unnecessary sensitive information through the public website enquiry form.

4. Sharing of Information

Falcon Investigations does not intend to sell personal information. Information may be shared where reasonably necessary to provide an agreed service, comply with law, protect legal rights, or respond to a lawful request. Information is handled with professional discretion and, where appropriate, on a need-to-know basis.

5. Data Security

We take reasonable organisational and technical precautions to protect information from unauthorised access, disclosure, alteration, or misuse. No internet transmission or storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

6. Retention

Information may be retained for as long as reasonably necessary for the purpose for which it was collected, business records, dispute resolution, legal obligations, or other legitimate purposes.

7. Your Requests

You may contact Falcon Investigations regarding questions or requests concerning personal information provided to us. Requests will be considered subject to applicable law, contractual obligations, investigation confidentiality, and legitimate legal requirements.

8. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time to reflect changes in services, technology, business practices, or applicable law.
